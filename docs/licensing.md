## Third-party material

The [WASI notice](../crates/sloper-extension-spec/wit/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
records interface provenance. Keep its
[license texts](../crates/sloper-extension-spec/wit/licenses/) with
redistributed interfaces.

CLI archives include `ThirdPartyNotices.txt`, generated by `cargo-license`.
It lists dependency names, versions, and declared licenses, excluding
development dependencies. It is a metadata report, not a bundle of full license
texts or an allowlist check.

Extension authors can supply the same report alongside their package's
`LICENSE`; `sloper-extension build` copies both into `dist`. Preserve applicable
upstream license texts and attribution notices with distributions.
